GOOD TO KNOW

Before you ride out.

  • Arrive early

    Gates open an hour before the main event; the procession waits for no one.

  • Bring your ticket

    Printed or on your phone. Your booking reference works at the gate too.

  • Access for all

    Step-free routes, companion seating and a quiet tent are available. Ask any steward.

  • Dress the part

    Costumes welcome. Blades stay sheathed and peace-tied in the stands.
