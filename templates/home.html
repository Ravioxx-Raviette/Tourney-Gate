A WORD FROM THE HERALD

Before the banners rise.

A few things to know
before your grand day out.

Is this real combat?+

No. Every event is fictional, staged entertainment with choreographed, safety-supervised performances. This website is a class demonstration.

How does seat reservation work?+

Choose your seats and continue to checkout. They are reserved for ten minutes while you complete your details. Expired reservations automatically return to availability.

Can I cancel a booking?+

Yes, up to 24 hours before the event begins. Use your email and reference in My Bookings. Cancelling returns your tickets to the box office.

Do I need to pay real money?+

No. All prices are for demonstration. Checkout uses a simulated payment and never collects card details.
